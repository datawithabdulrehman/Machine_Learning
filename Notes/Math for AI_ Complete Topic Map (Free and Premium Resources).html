Math for AI: The Complete Topic Map

Your full topic list, organized from beginner to advanced in 12 sections (72 modules), with free and premium resources for each. Work top to bottom. Sections 1, 2 and 4 can run side by side.

You can learn everything here for free. Premium picks are for structure, exercises, or a certificate. Many Coursera and edX courses can be audited free; you only pay for the certificate and graded work.

Mathematical Foundations

Beginner

Modules: Basic Operations, Algebraic Expressions, Functions, Coordinate Systems, Sigma Notation, Sets and Logic

Free

  • Khan Academy: Pre-algebra, Algebra 1 and 2, Precalculus
  • Desmos graphing calculator (plot every function you meet)
  • Khan Academy: Series and sigma notation
  • Trefor Bazett (YouTube): discrete math, sets and logic

Premium

  • Brilliant.org: Algebra, Functions, Logic courses
  • Coursera: Introduction to Mathematical Thinking (Stanford)
  • Book: Essential Math for Data Science (O'Reilly)
